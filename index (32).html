The Author

By day, a custodian.
By night, a world-builder.

Andrew Johnston didn't pick up a pen until twenty-five — before that, his creative hours went to drawing, not writing. It was tearing through George R.R. Martin's A Song of Ice and Fire that pulled him into fantasy for good, and into a community of fellow writers he still counts as friends.

In December 2023 he signed his debut novel, Primnoire, with Warrioress Publishing — the book that would become the first seed of the Iron Frost Universe. It was well received, earning him a handful of interviews along the way.

Since then, Andrew's path has turned independent. He's not ruling out traditional publishing again someday, but for now, the Iron Frost Universe is being built his own way — one self-published book at a time, from a home in Pennsylvania.
